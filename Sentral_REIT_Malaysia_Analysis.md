## Executive Summary

Sentral Real Estate Investment Trust (Sentral REIT) [SE:5] is a Malaysian commercial REIT primarily focused on office properties located in prime areas of Kuala Lumpur [SE:6]. The REIT's portfolio consists of 5 commercial buildings [SE:7] serving as corporate headquarters and office spaces with total assets of RM 2.80 billion [SE:8].

**Key Highlights (FY 2025):**
- **Portfolio Focus:** 5 commercial office properties in Kuala Lumpur [SE:8]
- **Gearing Ratio:** ~38.0% [SE:9] (comfortably below 60% regulatory limit [SE:10])
- **Interest Coverage:** ~3.2x [SE:11] (adequate debt servicing cushion)
- **2025 DPU:** 7.50 sen [SE:12] (up from 7.40 sen in FY 2024)
- **NAV per Unit:** RM 1.58 [SE:13] (declining trend from RM 1.65 in 2021)
- **Gross Revenue:** RM 180 million [SE:14]
- **Net Property Income:** RM 135 million [SE:14]
- **Occupancy Rate:** ~85% [SE:15]
- **WALE:** 6.5 years [SE:16]

## 8. Investment Thesis and Monitoring Plan

### 8.1 Investment Thesis

**Base Case View:**
Sentral REIT offers exposure to prime Kuala Lumpur commercial office properties with attractive current yield (~6.52% at RM 1.15) trading at significant discount to NAV (27%). The investment case depends on:
- KL office market stabilization and occupancy maintenance at ~85%+
- Long-term lease renewals with manageable rental reversions
- Interest rate environment stability
- Distribution sustainability at 7.0-7.5 sen range

**Key Considerations:**
- The deep discount to NAV (0.73x) reflects legitimate concerns about office sector outlook
- 100% KL concentration creates single-market risk
- 6.5-year WALE provides near-term income stability
- 38% conservative gearing provides financial flexibility
- High payout ratio (~98%) leaves limited buffer for income disruptions

### 8.2 Key Catalysts
| Catalyst | Impact | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Lease renewals | Income stability | Ongoing (WALE 6.5 years) |
| BNM policy changes | Finance cost impact | Monitor OPR decisions |
| Office market recovery | NAV recovery potential | Medium-term |
| Tenant retention | Occupancy maintenance | Ongoing |

### 8.3 Watch Items/Triggers
| Item | Trigger | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| **DPU Trend** | Decline below 7.0 sen | Review sustainability |
| **Occupancy** | Drop below 80% | Assess tenant risk |
| **Gearing** | Increase above 45% | Review debt profile |
| **NAV** | Further decline | Monitor revaluations |
| **Share Price** | Movement vs NAV discount | Reassess value proposition |

### 8.4 Conclusion

Sentral REIT provides exposure to prime commercial office properties in Kuala Lumpur with an attractive current yield (~6.52%) and significant discount to NAV (27%). However, investors should be aware of:

1. **100% KL geographic concentration** — single market risk with no diversification
2. **Challenging office sector outlook** — hybrid work trends affecting demand
3. **NAV decline trajectory** — from RM 1.65 (2021) to RM 1.58 (2025)
4. **High payout ratio (~98%)** — limited headroom for income disruptions
5. **85% occupancy** — below pre-pandemic levels; monitor lease renewals

**Suitable for:** Income-focused investors comfortable with office sector exposure and KL market concentration, seeking yield at discount to NAV.

**Not suitable for:** Investors requiring geographic diversification, growth-oriented strategies, or those concerned about office sector structural headwinds.
