## Executive Summary

### 2.2 Master Lease Structure (Key Risk/Opportunity Factor)

**What is a Master Lease?**
A master lease is a comprehensive lease agreement where an operator leases the entire hotel property from the REIT, paying rent based on a percentage of gross revenue and/or fixed base rent. This structure provides:
- ✅ Predictable income stream
- ✅ Transfer of operational risk to operator
- ✅ Professional management by hotel brands
- ⚠️ Revenue tied to operator performance

**YTL REIT Master Lease Arrangements:**
| Property Category | Lease Structure | Assessment |
|-------------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Malaysia Hotels** | Master leases with YTL Hotels/Operators [Y:80] | Related party transactions [Y:81] |
| **Australia Hotels** | Management agreements with Marriott [Y:82] | Professional operator |
| **Japan Hotels** | Various management structures [Y:83] | Operator-dependent |

**Critical Risk - Related Party Master Leases:**
Many Malaysian properties operate under master leases with YTL Group-related entities [Y:84]. This creates:
- 🔴 Potential conflict of interest in rent negotiations
- 🔴 Rent adjustments may favor operator over REIT
- 🔴 Less transparency in revenue allocation
- ✅ However, provides operational stability
