## Executive Summary

This Stage 2 analysis examines Pavilion REIT's lease profile, rental reversion trends, and occupancy patterns across 15 years of annual report data. The analysis covers five key themes: lease renewal/retention rates, rental reversion data, management's lease strategy commentary, historical occupancy trends by major asset, and lease expiry clustering versus subsequent occupancy outcomes.

**Key Finding:** Pavilion REIT's lease renewal track record **supports** its current Low risk rating, with the flagship Pavilion Kuala Lumpur Mall maintaining 95-99% occupancy for 15 consecutive years despite significant lease expiry clustering. The "3-year + 3-year option" lease reset strategy has effectively captured market rate growth, though rental reversions have moderated from 15% (2012-2013) to 4-5% (2017-2019) and stabilized post-COVID.

## Final Risk Assessment: Lease Renewal Track Record vs. Current Low Risk Rating

Pavilion REIT's lease renewal track record supports its current Low risk rating based on the following evidence: the flagship Pavilion KL Mall has maintained 95-99% occupancy for 15 consecutive years (2011-2025) despite lease expiry clustering as high as 74% in FY2013, demonstrating exceptional tenant demand and pricing power that insulates against renewal risk; rental reversions declined from 15% (2012-2013) to 4-5% (2017-2019) but stabilized at positive levels (4.4-4.5%) through 2017-2019, indicating the 3-year lease reset strategy effectively captures market rate growth, with the COVID-19 period (2020-2021) temporarily disrupting this pattern with flat renewals but 2024-2025 commentary suggesting improving negotiating position; WALE management shows discipline with voluntary reduction to 0.98 years in 2021 during market weakness and rebuilding to 1.43 years by 2024 as conditions improved, while the 2023 acquisition of Pavilion Bukit Jalil (RM2.2 billion) and 2025 hotel acquisitions further diversify lease expiry concentration risk; however, DA MEN Mall underperformance (62-73% occupancy 2021-2024 versus 95%+ for Pavilion KL) reveals that renewal risk is asset-specific rather than systemic, though the 2025 master tenancy arrangement for DA MEN (100% occupancy) addresses this through alternative leasing structures. The lease renewal track record provides evidence-based support for the Low risk rating, contingent on continued flagship asset performance and successful integration of newer assets into the portfolio.
