### 1.2 The Stapled Security Structure

**What is a Stapled Security?** [K:5]
A stapled security is a unique structure where two or more securities are contractually bound together and must be traded as a single unit. KLCCP Stapled Group combines:

| Component | Description | Assets Held |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **KLCC REIT** [K:6] | Shariah-compliant Islamic REIT | PETRONAS Twin Towers, Menara 3 PETRONAS, Menara ExxonMobil |
| **KLCC Property Holdings** [K:7] | Property development & investment co. | Suria KLCC (100%), Mandarin Oriental KL (75%), Menara Maxis (33%), Kompleks Dayabumi, Lot D1 |

**Advantages of Stapled Structure:** [K:29]
- ✅ Diversification across office, retail, and hospitality sectors
- ✅ Ability to hold non-REIT compliant assets (e.g., development land)
- ✅ Tax efficiency in distributions
- ✅ Unified governance and management
