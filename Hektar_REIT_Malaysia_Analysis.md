## Executive Summary

Hektar Real Estate Investment Trust (Hektar REIT) [H:5] is Malaysia's first retail-focused REIT and a pioneer in the Malaysian REIT market [H:6]. Listed on December 4, 2006 [H:7], the REIT operates a portfolio of 6 shopping centres and assets at December 2024 [H:8] (expanding to 7 with the February 2025 Penang Industrial acquisition), with a total asset value of RM 1,434.02 million [H:9]. The REIT has undergone significant strategic transformation in 2024-2025, diversifying beyond traditional retail with the acquisition of Kolej Yayasan Saad (educational asset) and a Penang industrial property [H:10].

**Key Highlights (FY 2024):**
- **Portfolio Composition:** 6 properties at Dec 2024 (5 retail malls + KYS), expanding to 7 in Feb 2025 with Penang Industrial acquisition [H:11]
- **Portfolio Occupancy:** 84.0% [H:12] (down from 86.7% in 2023 [H:13])
- **Gearing Ratio:** 41.72% [H:14] (below 60% regulatory limit)
- **Interest Coverage:** 1.66x [H:15] (concerning, near minimum threshold)
- **WALE:** 3.47 years [H:16] (moderate lease stability)
- **FY2024 DPU:** 3.15 sen [H:17] (lowest since 2021, due to tenant transition)
- **Gross Revenue:** RM 124.804 million [H:18] (+11.9% YoY [H:19])
- **Net Property Income (NPI):** RM 62.885 million [H:20] (+4.7% YoY [H:21])
- **Strategic Goal:** Double portfolio to RM 3.0 billion by 2027 [H:22]
- **Shariah Compliance:** Not Shariah-compliant [H:23]
