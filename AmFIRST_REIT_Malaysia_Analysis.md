## Executive Summary

AmFIRST Real Estate Investment Trust (AmFIRST REIT) [AF:5] is a Malaysia-based diversified REIT focused on commercial properties primarily located in the Klang Valley, with select assets in Melaka and Penang [AF:6]. As of March 31, 2026 [AF:7], the REIT maintains a portfolio of 8 commercial properties [AF:8] with a total net lettable area (NLA) of approximately 3.0 million square feet [AF:9].

**Key Highlights (FY 2026):**
- **Portfolio Occupancy:** 88.7% [AF:10] (improved from prior year)
- **Gearing Ratio:** 46.6% [AF:11] (below 60% regulatory limit [AF:12])
- **Interest Coverage:** Not disclosed in public filings
- **2026 Total DPU:** 2.87 sen [AF:13] (based on quarterly distributions)
- **NTA per Unit:** RM 1.2178 [AF:14]
- **Full Year Gross Revenue:** RM 110.3 million [AF:15] (+5.2% YoY)
- **Full Year NPI:** RM 64.1 million [AF:16] (+5.0% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax:** RM 30.4 million [AF:17] (+23.2% YoY)
