## Executive Summary

KIP Real Estate Investment Trust (KIP REIT) [KIP:5], operating under the KIPMalls brand [KIP:501], is a Malaysian retail REIT focused on neighborhood suburban shopping centers serving daily consumer needs [KIP:6]. As of June 30, 2025 [KIP:7], the REIT maintains a diversified portfolio of 18 properties [KIP:8] with total assets under management of ~RM 1.6 billion [KIP:9].

**Key Highlights (FY 2025):**
- **Portfolio Occupancy:** 96.7% [KIP:10] (strong suburban retail demand)
- **Gearing Ratio:** 39.88% [KIP:12] (comfortably below 60% regulatory limit [KIP:13])
- **Interest Coverage:** ~3.2x [KIP:14] (healthy coverage above 2.5x threshold)
- **2025 Total DPU:** 6.80 sen [KIP:15] (distributed across 4 payments [KIP:16])
- **NAV per Unit:** RM 1.1196 [KIP:17] (before final distribution)
- **Full Year Gross Revenue:** RM 136.13 million [KIP:18]
- **Full Year NPI:** RM 96.82 million [KIP:19]
- **Market Capitalization:** ~RM 824-829 million [KIP:20]
- **Profit After Tax:** RM 115.14 million [KIP:21]
