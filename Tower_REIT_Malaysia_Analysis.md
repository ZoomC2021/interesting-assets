## Executive Summary

Tower Real Estate Investment Trust (Tower REIT) [To:5] is a Malaysian commercial REIT focused on premium Grade A office properties primarily located in Kuala Lumpur's prime business districts [To:6]. As of June 30, 2024 [To:7], the REIT maintains a portfolio of 3 commercial office properties [To:8] with a total appraised value of RM 806 million [To:9].

**Key Highlights (FY 2024):**
- **Portfolio Occupancy:** Improved to highest levels in past five years; Menara Guoco achieved ~97% [To:10]
- **Gearing Ratio:** ~40.6% (estimated) [To:11] (below 50% regulatory limit [To:12])
- **Interest Coverage (estimated):** ~1.7x (external platform estimate) [To:13]
- **FY2024 Total DPU:** 0.77 sen (final distribution) [To:14] plus 0.23 sen interim = ~1.0 sen total [To:15]
- **NAV per Unit:** RM 1.16 [To:16]
- **Full Year Gross Revenue:** RM 37.04 million [To:17]
- **Full Year NPI:** RM 20.08 million [To:18]
- **Total Comprehensive Income:** RM 5.73 million (vs. loss of RM 17.60m in FY2023) [To:19]

### 7.2 Risk Detail Analysis

#### Interest Coverage Concern 🔶
- **Issue:** Estimated interest coverage ratio of ~1.7x is below the 2.0x+ comfort threshold observed in peer REITs
- **Implication:** Limited margin to absorb significant interest rate increases or income disruptions
- **Mitigation:** Conservative gearing (~40.6%) provides some headroom; Hong Leong Group backing may support favorable refinancing

#### Office Market Challenges 🔶
- **Issue:** KL office market faces oversupply headwinds and evolving workplace trends (hybrid work)
- **Implication:** Pressure on rental growth and occupancy sustainability
- **Mitigation:** Portfolio comprises Grade A premium assets in prime locations; refurbishment investments enhance competitiveness

## 8. Investment Thesis and Monitoring Plan

### 8.1 Investment Thesis

**Base Case View:**
Tower REIT offers exposure to three well-located Grade A office assets in Kuala Lumpur with the backing of an established sponsor (GuocoLand/Hong Leong Group). The significant financial turnaround in FY2024 (from loss to RM 5.7m profit) demonstrates operational improvement potential.

**Key Strengths:**
1. Premium asset locations in KLCC and Damansara Heights
2. Strong sponsor backing with development expertise
3. Conservative gearing with regulatory headroom
4. FY2024 operational turnaround with improved occupancy

**Key Concerns:**
1. Thin interest coverage (~1.7x estimated) limits rate increase resilience
2. 3-property concentration increases single-asset risk
3. Disclosure gaps (WALE, tenant concentration, debt profile) limit transparency
4. KL office market headwinds from oversupply and workplace evolution

### 8.2 Key Catalysts
| Catalyst | Direction | Probability |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Occupancy Sustained Above 95%** | Positive | Moderate |
| **Successful Debt Refinancing at Lower Rates** | Positive | Moderate |
| **KL Office Market Recovery** | Positive | Low-Moderate |
| **Further Rate Hikes** | Negative | Moderate |
| **Major Tenant Departure** | Negative | Unknown (data gap) |

### 8.3 Monitoring Checklist
| Item | Data Source | Frequency |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Quarterly occupancy rates | Bursa announcements | Quarterly |
| Debt refinancing updates | Annual Report / Announcements | Semi-annually |
| DPU declarations | Distribution announcements | Semi-annually |
| Interest coverage trend | Annual Report | Annually |
| Gearing ratio | Financial statements | Annually |

### 8.4 Triggers for Review
- Interest coverage falling below 1.5x
- Occupancy declining below 90% across portfolio
- Gearing exceeding 45%
- Any single tenant contributing >20% of gross rental income (if disclosed)
- Significant changes in KL office market supply/demand dynamics

### 8.5 Conclusion
Tower REIT represents a concentrated play on premium Kuala Lumpur office real estate with sponsor backing from GuocoLand. The FY2024 turnaround demonstrates management capability to improve operational performance. However, thin interest coverage and limited portfolio diversification warrant cautious position sizing. The REIT is suitable for investors seeking:
- Exposure to Grade A KL office assets
- Semi-annual income distributions
- Sponsor-backed management with development expertise
- Moderate risk tolerance for concentration and coverage metrics
