## Executive Summary

CapitaLand Malaysia Trust (CLMT) [C:9], formerly known as CapitaLand Malaysia Mall Trust (CMMT) [C:10], is Malaysia's premier diversified retail and logistics REIT [C:11]. Following a strategic rebranding in September 2021 [C:12], the REIT expanded beyond pure retail into logistics and industrial sectors [C:13], creating a resilient, multi-segment portfolio.

**Key Highlights (FY 2025):** [C:176]
- **Portfolio Size:** 14 properties [C:180] comprising 6 retail malls [C:15] and 8 logistics/industrial facilities
- **Total AUM:** RM 5.5 billion [C:188] (+7.0% YoY)
- **Market Capitalization:** ~RM 2.0-2.14 billion [C:19]
- **Total Units:** 2,879,966,000 units (~2.88 billion) [C:173]
- **Retail Occupancy:** 92.0% [C:21] with overall portfolio occupancy maintained strong
- **Gross Revenue:** RM 476.76 million [C:177] (+4.8% YoY)
- **Net Property Income (NPI):** RM 289.44 million [C:178] (+9.7% YoY) - highest since listing
- **Distributable Income:** Growing income pool supporting distributions
- **DPU:** 4.84 sen [C:179] (+4.1% YoY)
- **Gearing:** 39.0% [C:189] (improved from 41.3%)
- **NAV:** RM 0.9370 per unit pre-distribution [C:190]
- **Rental Reversions:** Stable positive reversions
- **Shopper Traffic:** Continued growth momentum
