## Executive Summary

Atrium Real Estate Investment Trust (Atrium REIT) [T:5] is a Malaysian industrial REIT focused on warehouse and manufacturing properties primarily located in the Klang Valley (Selangor) and Penang [T:6]. As of December 31, 2025 [T:7], the REIT maintains a portfolio of 9 industrial properties [T:8] with a total asset value of RM 723.24 million [T:9].

**Key Highlights (FY 2025):**
- **Portfolio Occupancy:** 100% [T:10] (all properties fully occupied as of Q4 2025) [T:11]
