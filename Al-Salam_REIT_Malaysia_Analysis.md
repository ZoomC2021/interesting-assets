## Executive Summary

Al-Salam Real Estate Investment Trust (Al-Salam REIT) [L:5] is a Malaysia-based, Shariah-compliant REIT listed on Bursa Malaysia's Main Market [L:6]. Established in 2015 [L:7], the REIT manages a diversified portfolio of 54 properties [L:8] with a total asset value of approximately RM1.24 billion [L:9]. The portfolio spans four key segments: retail (flagship KOMTAR JBCC), office (Menara KOMTAR), F&B restaurants (42 KFC and Pizza Hut outlets under triple-net lease), and industrial/educational assets [L:10].

**Key Highlights (FY 2025):**
- **Portfolio Properties:** 54 assets across Malaysia [L:11]
- **Total Asset Value:** RM1.24 billion [L:12]
- **Portfolio Occupancy:** ~69% (Q1 2025) [L:13]; F&B segment at 100% [L:14]
- **Gearing Ratio:** ~48.6% (FY2024) [L:15], within SC Malaysia's 50% regulatory limit [L:16]
- **2025 Total DPU:** 2.20 sen [L:17] (214% increase from 0.70 sen in FY2024) [L:18]
- **NAV per Unit:** RM1.1241 (before final distribution) [L:19]
- **Full Year Gross Revenue:** RM88.83 million [L:20] (+13.2% YoY)
- **Full Year NPI:** RM61.10 million [L:21] (+18.9% YoY)
- **Shariah Compliance:** Certified Shariah-compliant structure [L:22]
