## Executive Summary

Axis Real Estate Investment Trust (Axis REIT) [A:6] is Malaysia's premier diversified industrial REIT and the larger peer to Atrium REIT [A:7]. Celebrating its 20th anniversary on Bursa Malaysia in 2025 [A:5], Axis REIT manages a portfolio of **70 properties** [A:8] (following a Port Klang acquisition completed in late January 2026) with total assets under management (AUM) of approximately **RM 5.36 billion** [A:9] and net lettable area of ~15 million sq ft [A:10]. The REIT is Shariah-compliant [A:11], making it eligible for Islamic investment mandates.

**Key Highlights (FY 2025):** [A:3]
- **Portfolio Size:** 70 properties [A:8] across 6 states [A:12] (Klang Valley, Johor, Penang, Kedah, Pahang) [A:13]
- **Portfolio Occupancy:** 94% [A:14] with WALE of 4.4 years [A:15]

### 2.3 Property Enhancement (AEI) Activity

**What is AEI?**  
**AEI (Asset Enhancement Initiative)** refers to capital investments made to improve, upgrade, or modernize existing properties to:
- ✅ Maintain competitiveness in the industrial market
- ✅ Attract and retain quality tenants (MNCs, logistics operators)
- ✅ Increase rental income potential and property valuations
- ✅ Extend the useful life of aging assets (reset "effective age")
- ✅ Meet evolving tenant specifications (automation, sustainability, floor loadings)

**2025 AEI Activity:**
| Period | Capex Investment | Purpose |
|--------|------------------|---------|
| **Q1 2025** | RM 4.51 million [A:122] | Property enhancements + maintenance |
| **Q2 2025** | RM 1.88 million (capex) [A:123] + RM 6.6M (leasehold extension) [A:124] | Asset improvements |
| **Total 2025** | ~RM 13 million+ [A:125] | Maintaining competitiveness |

**Historical AEI Examples (Portfolio Modernization):**
| Property | AEI Period | Nature of Works | Outcome |
|----------|------------|-----------------|---------|
| **Bukit Raja Distribution Centre 2** | 2021-2023 | Purpose-built development for SPX Xpress | Modern logistics facility (620K sq ft) |
| **Various legacy properties** | Ongoing | Roof replacements, floor slab upgrades | Extended useful life |
| **Green certifications** | Multi-year | GBI/GreenRE sustainability upgrades | ESG compliance, tenant demand |

**AEI Strategy vs Atrium REIT:**
| Aspect | Axis REIT Approach | Atrium REIT Approach |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Scale** | ~RM 13M+ annually (2025) | RM 34.4M (2025, focused on ASA5) |
| **Focus** | Brownfield redevelopment + development | Property-by-property refurbishment |

| **Portfolio Impact** | Continuous refresh across 70 properties | Targeted enhancement of 9 properties |

**Assessment:**
- **Consistent Investment:** ~RM 13M+ annual capex maintains property quality across the 69-property portfolio
- **Strategic AEI:** Leasehold extension (RM 6.6M) secures long-term tenure for key assets
- **Brownfield Focus:** Unlike Atrium's pure refurbishment approach, Axis combines AEIs with active capital recycling (disposals + new acquisitions)
- **Development Edge:** Build-to-suit capability (BRDC 2) adds modern, purpose-built assets that don't require future AEIs
- **ESG Integration:** Green certifications serve as proxy for asset modernity and quality

**Investor Implication:** Axis REIT's AEI strategy is more **scalable and systematic** than Atrium's given the larger portfolio. The combination of AEIs, development capability, and capital recycling provides multiple avenues for portfolio refresh—reducing reliance on any single approach.
